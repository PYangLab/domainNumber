*[ARI]: Adjusted Rand index: agreement between two groupings of the spots. 1 = identical, about 0 = no better than chance.
*[NMI]: Normalized mutual information: agreement between two groupings of the spots, from 0 to 1.
*[BIC]: Bayesian information criterion: scores how well a model fits and penalizes extra parameters.
*[HMRF]: Hidden Markov random field: a model in which neighboring spots tend to share a domain.
*[MCMC]: Markov chain Monte Carlo: fits a Bayesian model by repeated random sampling.
*[GMM]: Gaussian mixture model.
*[DLPFC]: Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex.
*[UMI]: Unique molecular identifier: one counted transcript.
*[WM]: White matter.
